DEAD OF NIGHT
Harbor Town · four survivors · one way out

The radio said the docks. Get out of the safe room, cross the town, the park and the warehouse, and call the boat. Stick together. Don't startle the crying woman.

Difficulty

Mouse sensitivity 1.00

Volume 80%

How to survive

  • W A S D move · Mouse look · Space jump · C/Ctrl crouch · Shift walk
  • LMB fire · RMB shove — knocks back anything grabbing you · R reload
  • 1 primary · 2 pistols · 3 pipe bomb / molotov · 4 first aid (hold LMB) · 5 pills
  • E pick up · open doors · hold to revive a downed teammate · use the radio
  • F flashlight · Tab team status · Esc pause

Know the infected

  • Hunter — screeches, then pounces and pins you. Teammates shove or shoot it off.
  • Boomer — bloated. Its bile draws the horde. Don't pop it up close.
  • Tank — you'll hear it before you see it. Run, burn it, unload everything.
  • Witch — you'll hear her crying. Lights off, keep your distance, don't shoot.
